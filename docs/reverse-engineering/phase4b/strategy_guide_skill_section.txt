Earning Skill Points
At the end of each level you will be awarded Skill points based on an assessment of how well
you played that level. As well, the tween-level music and exclamation will vary depending on
the Skill points earned.
The calculation for skill points is based on a number of factors. The following increase the skill
points awarded:
• Difficulty of the level
• Leaving Munch dots uneaten (the more the better)
• Leaving Yuk dots uneaten at end of level
• Always eating at least one bug with each Munch dot
• Special power left at end of level
• Not activating a Yummy power
• Eating all goodie dots
• Not eating any goodie dots (actually give more than eating all of them)
• Eating Yummy, Bonus and Multiplier dots later in their life cycle
• Eating Yuk dots earlier
And these decrease skill points:
• aquadot!red taking damage
• aquadot!red dying
Of course, not all of these factors are given equal weight. As well, some of them are of the all-or-
nothing variety, such as "not activating a Yummy power", whereas most of them have a range of
values. For instance, leaving one Munch dot at the end of the level doesn't increase your skill
much, but leaving all of them increases it greatly.
Tip: The best way to earn Skill points is to avoid taking damage and barring that, not letting
aqauadot!red die at all. The next best way is to avoid eating Munch dots.
