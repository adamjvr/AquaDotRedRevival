Bug Strategy Wanders slowly and randomly in areas where there are uneaten
normal dots. If aquadot!red eats any dots in the vicinity however,
Protector will get angry and pursue relentlessly.
Defense Try to avoid eating dots around Protector. If you need to eat dots,
“Protector”
then try to do so behind him as he won't always notice dots being
eaten there. If Protector starts chasing you, run into an area cleared
of dots and eventually he will give up and start protecting the
remaining dots again.

“Mantis”

“Hermit”
